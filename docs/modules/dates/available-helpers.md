# Available Helpers

- `gen_dates_list`: build a daily, weekly, or monthly sequence
- `first_day`: first day of a week or month
- `last_day`: last day of a week or month
- `add_days`
- `add_weeks`
- `add_months`
- `add_quarters`
- `sanitize_date`: convert a date to `YYYYMMDD`
